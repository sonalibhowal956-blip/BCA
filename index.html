Institutional Overview

About Department of Computer Applications

Fostering excellence in computational sciences, software architecture, and ethical computing since 2004.

Vision & Mission

Department Vision

To be a nationally recognized center of undergraduate education and applied innovation in Computer Applications, producing software innovators and researchers committed to technological excellence and societal betterment.

Department Mission

  • Provide a robust academic environment that balances theoretical computer science with agile industry practices.
  • Equip students with strong algorithmic problem solving, software engineering, and artificial intelligence capabilities.
  • Encourage collaborative research, hackathons, open-source development, and entrepreneurial ventures.
  • Instill ethical leadership, cyber safety awareness, and lifelong learning attitudes in all graduates.

Laboratory Infrastructure

Our department houses three state-of-the-art computing laboratories equipped with dual-boot Linux/Windows workstations, gigabit local networking, and cloud access to AWS Educate and Azure Dev Tools.

Lab 1: Software Systems & Web Engineering

Node.js, Python, Java, Docker, and full stack development environments.

Lab 2: AI & Data Engineering

GPU-accelerated systems for machine learning, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, and database clustering.
